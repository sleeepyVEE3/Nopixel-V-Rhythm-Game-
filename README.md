browser-based rhythm minigame, styled after the hacking/lockpicking minigames on the NoPixel V RP server. 

Notes slide in from the right across three lanes — hit A, S, or D as each one crosses its lane's hit line.

How to launch:
Open nopixel-v-rhythm-game.html in any browser (no install needed).
Pick a difficulty mode, then click Start.
